 Lecture-Organizer-Tree-
Local-first botanical lecture organizer and study sanctuary. Organize courses as interactive oak trees, topic branches, and verbatim PDF/PPTX leaves in IndexedDB—complete with true-to-slide readers, offline NLP summaries, active-recall quizzes, and cozy ambient audio.
 
 Botanical Course Architecture
Subject Trees & Topic Branches:** Organize multiple courses by subject code and title, branching out into modular lecture units.
Non-Destructive IndexedDB Binary Vault:** Uploaded `.PDF` and `.PPTX` binaries are stored 100% verbatim in browser `IndexedDB` with **SHA-256 hash verification**, keeping original slides, typography, and layouts completely untouched.
Portable `.JSON` Snapshots:** Save your workspace locally or export and import full `.json` archives for backup or transfer across devices.

 Built-In Document & Slide Readers
 rue-to-Slide PPTX Presentation Reader:** Client-side OpenXML (`.pptx`) parser powered by JSZip with coordinate-mapped rendering, slide filmstrip, grid view, speaker notes drawer, laser pointer, and fullscreen stage mode.
azy-Rendered PDF Viewer:** Powered by `PDF.js` with single-page and continuous-scroll modes, zoom presets, 90-degree rotation, page thumbnails, and native browser PDF fallback.

 Offline Study & Active Recall
Pipi — Archival Companion:** An interactive sparrow assistant that generates extractive executive digests, builds custom study reviewers, lists decks, compares lecture leaves side-by-side, and answers questions from indexed lecture text.
Treehouse Subject Overview:** A centralized dashboard for executive subject synthesis, lecture highlights, custom Core Concepts per branch, and document management.
Automated Quiz Generator:** Builds offline active-recall quizzes directly from your imported lecture slides and PDFs.

 Cozy Study Sanctuary
Mochi — Study Buddy:** An animated vector companion featuring daily streak/XP tracking and motivational prompts.
Dynamic Weather & Procedural Audio:** Switch between Clear, Autumn Leaves, Rain, Snow, or Auto-Cycle weather with wind physics, performance modes, and a built-in Web Audio ambient synthesizer (plus custom audio file upload).
